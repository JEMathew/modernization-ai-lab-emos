# Modernization AI Lab — Three-Minute Demo

> **Modernization AI Lab is an Enterprise Modernization Operating System (EMOS) that transforms enterprise strategy and evidence into governed modernization decisions and outcomes.**

**Live demo:** [Launch Modernization AI Lab](https://modernization-ai-lab-emos.jeasom.workers.dev/)

The public experience uses the fictional Apex Aerospace enterprise and synthetic data only. Its scoring, canonical 6R recommendations, workflow transitions, and validation outcomes are deterministic. AI-specialist collaboration is represented by scripted responses; the public build does not make live LLM, Codex, cloud, or enterprise-system calls.

## Demo objective

Show one uninterrupted path from enterprise evidence to a canonical 6R recommendation and a visible human-governance hard-stop for one modernization unit.

## Primary demo path

| Time | Presenter action | Story to communicate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0:00–0:20** | Frame the problem before opening the product. | Enterprise modernization decisions are fragmented across inventories, evidence, dependencies, owners, risk, and business priorities. Leaders need to know what to modernize, why it matters, and what must be governed before action. |
| **0:20–0:40** | Open the [live demo](https://modernization-ai-lab-emos.jeasom.workers.dev/). | EMOS organizes a shared modernization case and its work objects. It is an operating model for evidence-backed decisions, not a chatbot or an autonomous deployment tool. |
| **0:40–1:05** | Select **Try Sample Enterprise**, review the **Executive Engagement Brief**, then select **Enter Mission Control**. | Apex Aerospace provides a deterministic, synthetic portfolio of ten modernization units so a judge can experience the product immediately. |
| **1:05–2:10** | Focus on the **Customer Intelligence Capability** and its **Customer Analytics Warehouse** modernization unit. Inspect the evidence and dependency context, then follow the highlighted **Immediate Next Action** until the assessment and recommendation are visible. | EMOS connects the Oracle warehouse, its business importance, modernization pressure, and Finance Warehouse dependency. Deterministic assessment produces a canonical 6R recommendation of **Replatform**, with **58.5% confidence** and **Trust: Blocked**. The recommendation and supporting evidence remain separately inspectable. |
| **2:10–2:35** | Show the case at **Decision Pending** and inspect the blocker. | The system does not silently progress. Twelve Finance Warehouse-dependent reports and unresolved ownership/change authority trigger a governance hard-stop. The Mission Commander must decide whether those reports must remain unchanged for the first six months. **No execution authority is granted.** |
| **2:35–2:50** | Return to the portfolio context in Mission Control. | The same evidence, dependency, recommendation, and governance model is demonstrated across ten connected sample units. This is a bounded synthetic portfolio demonstration, not a claim of production-scale ingestion. |
| **2:50–3:00** | Close on the product direction. | Today the demo proves **Evidence → Assessment → 6R Recommendation → Governed Decision**. The future vision extends this foundation into governed portfolio planning, authorized execution through adapters, deployment validation, benefit realization, and continuous learning. |

## Judge takeaways

- The modernization unit—not chat—is the primary work object.
- Numeric assessment and 6R selection are deterministic and reproducible.
- Recommendations retain evidence, confidence, trust state, and decision lineage.
- A blocked trust state creates a visible human decision gate rather than autonomous progression.
- Mission Control keeps portfolio context and the active modernization case synchronized.

## Demonstration boundaries

- All enterprise names, records, evidence, and outcomes are synthetic.
- The browser experience is a deterministic product prototype.
- AI-specialist dialogue and generated artifact contents are mocked or scripted.
- Runtime activation, autonomous modernization, live deployment, and benefit-realization automation are future capabilities, not demonstrated production behavior.
